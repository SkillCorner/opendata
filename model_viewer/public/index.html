Pitch Model Lab

v0 vs v2 pitch control, EPV and defensive integrity · SkillCorner open data, A-League 2024/25 · 10 Hz

v0 · literature settings, location value

Pitch control uses settings borrowed from the literature: 0.5 s reaction, 15 m/s ground pass, σ = 0.45, 12 m/s lofted pass with 0.6 s hang time. The value of a spot is SkillCorner's possession EPV smoothed by location only.

v2 · fitted settings, defensive-context value

The same pitch-control model with 10 settings fitted on 11,110 passes from 15 training matches (held-out AUC 0.885, calibrated). The value of a spot is the location value × a multiplier for the defensive situation there: how fast the nearest defender arrives, how close they are, and how many defenders are goal-side.

Reading the layers

Pitch control: chance the attacking team gets to the ball first (Patt) or that a pass there is completed (Psucc, with interception along the path and the lofted option). EPV: value of having the ball at each spot. Gain: Psucc·V − (1−Psucc)·Vopp − V(ball), what a pass there is worth. Integrity: how well the defence denies passes into the space from 5 m behind the ball to goal, I = Σ V·(1−Psucc) / Σ V. The map shows 1−Psucc in that zone, faded where the space is worth little. Surfaces are always from the attacking team's point of view.

Caveats

No receiver is known on a free-playing frame, so run credit is not applied. The v2 value was fitted on possession-start points and is applied here to every cell. Five matches (marked held-out) were not used to fit anything.
